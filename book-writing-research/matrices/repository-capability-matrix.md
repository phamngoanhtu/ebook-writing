## Capability Breakdown Summary

1. **Ideation & Audience**: `writing-template-for-ai`, `bookgen`, `KDP-Publishing-Prompt-Library`, and `agentic-novel-outliner` provide the strongest commercial and positioning validation frameworks.
2. **Architecture & Outlining**: `writing-template-for-ai`, `speckit-preset-fiction-book-writing`, `novel-template`, and `boekwriter` establish the most structured hierarchical breakdown methods.
3. **Drafting & Scene Execution**: `writing-template-for-ai` (fractal scene circuits), `boekwriter` (chunk budgets), and `AI_Novel` (genre style injection) lead in prose generation precision.
4. **Context & Memory Management**: `writing-template-for-ai` (`MEMORY.md` + `CHANGELOG_AI.md`), `novel-template` (modular character/world folders), and `speckit-preset-fiction-book-writing` (`constitution.md`) represent the state of the art in persistent context control.
5. **Editing & Quality Control**: `The-Novelists-Atelier` (22-pass editorial analysis), `writing-template-for-ai` (6 quality gates + prose suppression), and `speckit-preset-fiction-book-writing` (dedicated continuity & pacing commands) provide exhaustive multi-pass quality control.
6. **Publishing & Assembly**: `writing-template-for-ai` (Pandoc EPUB/PDF), `boekwriter` (LaTeX/PDF with SVG/tables), `bookgen` (BookOps Makefile + KDP packaging), and `LLM-book-generator` (Python DOCX builder) offer verified compilation engines.
